# Personal website

Riley Meredith's personal site: a paper landing page, section hubs, and destination pages.

## Language

**Landing**:
The navbar-free home page at `/`.
_Avoid_: Marketing page, splash

**Interior**:
Any non-home route; shares one light navbar shell.
_Avoid_: App section, chrome routes (as the product name)

**Hub**:
An interior index page that routes into destinations (`/projects`, `/words`).
_Avoid_: Section home, category page

**Destination page**:
A leaf page reached from a hub or landing tile (e.g. Hoops Lab, Blog, Books). Redesign of existing destination pages is out of scope for the current wayfinder map.
_Avoid_: Subpage (when you mean the leaf, not the hub)

**Paper**:
The site-wide light ground color `#f4f1ea` and its matching ink/chrome treatment.
_Avoid_: Cream, off-white, light mode (as the token name)
